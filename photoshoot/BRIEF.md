# Vaigai Milk: photoshoot brief (farm + product)

Goal: replace the AI pictures with real photos that match the site look (fresh green and teal, soft natural light, clean, Madurai local). Real photos raise trust, conversion and Google image ranking.

## Plan (2 days)
| Day | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 morning (5:30 to 9 AM) | Farm and dairy | Cattle, milking, dairy, chilling, bottling, the temple tower in the background if visible, team portraits |
| 1 afternoon | Dairy | Packaging line, quality testing, lab, packing crates |
| 2 morning (5 to 7 AM) | Delivery route | Rider loading, doorstep delivery, crate, brass pot, filter coffee pour |
| 2 daytime | Studio or clean white / green wall | All product hero shots, splash shots, flat lays, recipes |

## Gear options
- Budget (about Rs 8,000 to 15,000 per day): a photographer with a mirrorless camera, 2 softboxes, reflector, tripod, plus a phone for video clips.
- Premium (Rs 25,000 to 60,000 per day): product + lifestyle photographer with food stylist, splash photography (high-speed flash) and a 30 to 60 second brand film.
- Ask for RAW or high-quality JPG, minimum 4000 px on the long side.

## Look and feel
- Colours: deep green, teal, cream, a little gold. Avoid yellow cast.
- Light: soft window light or large softboxes; for farm, golden hour (first 45 minutes after sunrise).
- Backgrounds: for products shoot on a plain seamless backdrop (green, teal or white) so I can cut them out cleanly. Include one frame of the empty backdrop.
- Style: fresh, condensation droplets on cold bottles, clean labels facing the camera, natural props only (brass pot, banana leaf, steel tumbler, jasmine, cardamom, coffee beans, almonds).
- People: real farmers and riders; get a signed photo-release from everyone who is recognisable.

## Rules
- Labels must show the real FSSAI licence number and legal text as on the real pack (check with your CA / label designer).
- No stock logos, no other brands in frame.
- Keep the original files; never edit labels in photos.
- Shoot every product both with and without splash.

## Delivery
Name files exactly as in `SHOT-LIST.csv` (column `filename`). Put them in one folder and run `python ingest.py raw_folder out_folder`. It makes the website-ready WebP files and the alt text list. Send me the folder and I will rebuild the cut-outs, floating particles and hero carousel from your photos.
